Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026 • Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Core Privacy Commitment: NumRiddle is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location, and never sell personal data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how NumRiddle: Offline Math & Logic Quest ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles information when you install and play our application on Android-compatible devices.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

NumRiddle is engineered with an offline-first architecture. The following data is saved exclusively in your device's private local application sandbox (via Android SharedPreferences):

  • Completed stage numbers (Levels 1 to 120)
  • Best completion times per puzzle (in seconds)
  • Earned stars and current player rank status
  • Remaining hint count
  • User audio and vibration preferences (Sound ON/OFF, Haptics ON/OFF)

This data remains strictly on your device. You can erase all game data at any time through the in-game Settings menu ("Reset Game Progress") or via your device's application settings ("Clear Storage / Clear Data").

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To support ongoing development while keeping the Game free, NumRiddle integrates the Google AdMob advertising service provided by Google LLC.

When your device is connected to the internet, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / Amazon Advertising Identifier) subject to your device settings.
  • Device and Telemetry Data: IP address (used for approximate geographic country/region ad delivery), device make and model, operating system version, and mobile network status.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, video completion events (for rewarded hints), and technical error logs.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

NumRiddle is designed as a general-audience brain-training and puzzle application suitable for all age groups. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been unintentionally collected, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete it.

5. Opt-Out and Managing Personalized Ads

Players can opt out of interest-based and personalized advertising directly through their device operating system settings:

  • Amazon Fire OS / Android Devices: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or tap "Delete Advertising ID".
  • When opted out, you will continue to receive the same number of ads, but ads will be contextual rather than personalized.

6. Security

We value your privacy. Because NumRiddle does not operate external user account databases, your gameplay statistics and progress cannot be exposed through server breaches.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new platform requirements, or statutory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
